What we do

Commercial cleaning built for turnover

Post-construction and facility services for GCs and property teams across Dallas–Fort Worth.

Rough Cleaning

Performed during deep construction — clearing bulk debris and coarse dust while the build is still underway so finishes can move forward cleanly.

Final Cleaning

Once construction is complete — a thorough turnover clean so the space is finished, inspected, and ready for occupants. Most GCs book final, then puff.

Puff Cleaning (Powder Puff)

A lighter detail clean after the punch walk from other trades — lifting fine dust so the space is truly presentable.

Carpet Cleaning

Commercial carpet cleaning using hot water steam, rotary bonnet, or extraction methods. Removes dirt, stains, and construction residue for a cleaner finish.

Window Cleaning (Interior & Exterior)

Streak-free glass inside and out for storefronts, offices, and commercial openings.

Warehouse Machine Cleaning

Equipment-assisted cleaning for large warehouse and industrial floor areas where push mops aren’t enough.

VCT Strip & Wax

Strip, prepare, and recoat VCT floors for a durable, professional finish in commercial spaces.

Hard Floor Care

Cleaning, polishing, and maintenance for tile, concrete, and other hard surfaces beyond VCT.

How we work

A clear path from walkthrough to turnover

We start by understanding what you need, then deliver the right cleans in the right order — so GCs and PMs can plan with confidence.

1. Walkthrough

We walk the site first to understand what you want — scope, schedule, access, and how the space should look at handover.

2. Tailored proposal

You receive a proposal built around your project — the right mix of rough, final, and puff, sized to your timeline.

3. Rough cleaning

During deep construction we clear bulk debris and coarse dust so finishes and following trades can keep moving.

4. Final cleaning

When construction is done, we deliver a thorough final clean so the space is move-in ready. Most GCs book this first, then puff.

5. Puff cleaning

After the punch walk from other trades, a lighter detail pass lifts fine dust and leaves the space truly presentable.

FAQ

Questions GCs and facilities ask

What areas do you serve in DFW?

We serve Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding areas within about a 60-mile radius, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Carrollton, and nearby communities.

What’s the difference between rough, puff, and final cleaning?

Rough cleaning happens during deep construction — bulk debris and coarse dust while the build is underway. Final cleaning is the thorough turnover pass once construction is complete. Puff (powder puff) cleaning is a lighter detail clean after the punch walk from other trades. Most GCs schedule final, then puff.

Do you strip and wax VCT floors?

Yes. We strip, prepare, and recoat VCT for a durable commercial finish.

Do you offer warehouse machine cleaning?

Yes. We provide equipment-assisted cleaning for large warehouse and industrial floor areas.

Do you clean windows inside and out?

Yes. We clean commercial windows on both the interior and exterior.

Do you clean carpets?

Yes. We offer commercial carpet cleaning using hot water steam, rotary bonnet, or extraction methods.

Are your cleaning services eco-friendly?

Yes, we prioritize eco-certified products and sustainable practices in all our cleanups.
